# External positioning feedback (source: ChatGPT critique, 2026-09-26)

Working reference for the `redesign` branch. Not published — lives outside `src/` and `public/` so it never gets built into the site.

## Core thesis to reposition around
> I build and lead the teams, systems and platform foundations that help complex product organizations scale.

Narrowed direction: **Product design leader for complex platforms — building capable teams, scalable systems, and strong design-engineering practices.** AI should support this story, not compete with it as a separate specialty.

Two target lanes for $200K+ roles:
1. Product Design Manager/Senior Manager — platform, enterprise, or infrastructure products.
2. Principal/Lead Platform or Design Systems roles with explicit team leadership / player-coach responsibility (currently the faster route in — platform/systems proof is strong, large-org management scope is still developing).

## Where the story is currently disconnected

**Portfolio**
- 4 of 5 projects are from Modern Health or Stitch Fix; strongest leadership evidence (Tive — managing senior designers, design ops, design system, changing how teams work) is absent.
- Most projects show design execution, not how direction was set, people developed, executives influenced, or an org improved.
- Medium articles / old Figma presentations add friction, feel assembled from older artifacts.
- "My Design Journey" is thoughtful but longer than hiring managers need.
- Net effect: reads as "excellent former platform IC," not "current design leader."

**Resume**
- Same themes (platforms, systems, AI, scaling, leadership, eng partnership) appear everywhere — dilutes the distinctive advantage.
- Tive section mostly describes activities (lead, introduce, define, partner, establish) — lacks organizational change, adoption, business results, before/after evidence.
- AI language is current-sounding but abstract, not differentiating.
- Strongest quantitative evidence is buried in older roles; Stitch Fix progression is compressed and undersells its significance.

**LinkedIn**
- Headline ("Product Design Leader | Design Systems & Platforms | AI-Enabled Product Development | Design + Engineering") bundles several ideas instead of one memorable identity.

## What to lean into

**Portfolio**
- The rare combo: design leadership + systems thinking + real front-end engineering credibility.
- Complex internal/enterprise products, not just polished consumer UI.
- Stitch Fix progression: designer → design engineer → platform leader.
- New flagship **Tive case study** — about changing the team and operating model, not shipping a feature.
- Ability to create leverage: better designers, reusable patterns, stronger cross-functional decisions, faster learning.

**Resume**
- Measurable systems results: 40%→90% adoption, 150 components, accessibility improvement, multi-team scope.
- Career progression within Stitch Fix.
- Specific management evidence: who was managed, how their ownership changed, hiring/coaching, what improved.
- Repeated pattern of building foundations in ambiguous environments.

**LinkedIn**
- Sharper About section built around the leadership thesis.
- Featured links: one current leadership case study + one strong platform/design-system case study.
- Occasional posts on scaling design quality, player-coach leadership, design-engineering collaboration.

## Gaps to address
- Recent leadership evidence (Tive) isn't represented anywhere yet.
- Management scale: team of 3 is credible for some roles, but Senior Manager roles at larger companies may expect more (managers reporting up, workforce planning, broader org ownership) — needs framing.
- Shorter recent tenures (Anomalie, Modern Health, Built) may read as movement unless briefly contextualized (acquisition/layoff/org circumstances).
- Business impact: strong operational/design-system outcomes, but thin connective tissue to revenue, retention, cost, delivery speed, or strategic priorities.
- Executive influence: leadership is claimed but rarely demonstrated via a specific hard organizational decision driven across Product/Eng.
- Current craft: portfolio leans on older work — reviewers may wonder what's designed today.
- Title/claim ambiguity: phrases like "managed platform design organization" need unmistakable context (formal reports, team composition, actual authority).

## Working implication for this redesign
Tive becomes the lead story. Not "managed projects" — "improved the system through which an entire team delivers design." Reduce the older Modern Health/Stitch Fix-heavy project mix's dominance, or reframe those pieces as evidence supporting (not carrying) the leadership thesis.
